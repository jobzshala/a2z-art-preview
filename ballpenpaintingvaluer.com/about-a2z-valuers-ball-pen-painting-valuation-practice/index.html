A2Z Valuers — Ball Pen Painting Valuation Specialists

The specialist practice

A2Z Valuers is a Delhi-headquartered valuation practice led by Nitesh Shrivastava — Government Approved Valuer. Within its extensive painting medium and technique valuation network — covering 50+ distinct media across the paintingvaluers.com network — the ball pen painting valuation practice occupies a distinct specialist position. Ball pen and ballpoint pen as fine art media sit at the intersection of drawing and painting, demand medium-specific technical knowledge for condition assessment, and present comparables challenges that do not arise in better-documented historical media. A2Z Valuers addresses these challenges with a dedicated ball pen painting valuation protocol that applies the practice’s Government Approved Valuer credential to a medium that most generalist valuers approach without adequate specialist preparation.

Nitesh Shrivastava

  • Nitesh Shrivastava
  • Civil Engineer | Government Approved Valuer | Proprietor, A2Z Valuers

Nitesh Shrivastava is a Civil Engineer by training and a Government Approved Valuer by profession with over two decades of artwork valuation practice. His ball pen painting valuation practice applies the same rigorous, statute-compliant methodology that the practice uses across all painting media — adapted to the specific technical demands of oil-based ballpoint ink on paper or card supports: ink chemistry assessment, oxidation and yellowing evaluation, support interaction analysis, scale and labour documentation, hyper-realism technical complexity assessment, and contemporary market comparables research. His Government Approved Valuer credential — registered under Section 34AB of the Income Tax Act and the suite of Indian statutory registrations — means that every ball pen painting valuation report he issues is accepted by the Indian authorities that matter: the Income Tax Department, NCLT, the High Court, nationalised banks, and art insurers.
