From First Contact to Signed Certificate — The Ball Pen Painting Valuation Process

01

Initial Brief

A confidential conversation about the ball pen painting — the artist (known or unknown), the approximate dimensions, the subject matter, the approximate date of creation, the known provenance, and the purpose of the valuation (insurance, capital gain, estate, auction, litigation, donation). We confirm acceptance, identify the applicable statutory framework, and indicate a fee and timeline.

02

Engagement Letter

Written engagement letter setting out scope, statutory framework, basis of value (replacement cost for insurance, fair market value for capital gain and estate), deliverables, fee, and turnaround. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

03

Medium Protocol Confirmation

Before physical inspection, the ball pen painting – specific valuation protocol is confirmed in writing: the ink chemistry assessment procedure, the scale and labour documentation approach, the hyper-realism premium framework (where applicable), the comparables sources to be used, and the drawing-vs-painting classification to be applied. This confirmation ensures that the inspection step is targeted and efficient.

04

Physical Examination

Physical examination of the ball pen painting in situ. Photography under standard light, raking light, and UV light (where warranted). Ink chemistry assessment — colour stability, migration, yellowing. Support condition assessment — paper grade, acidity indicators, physical damage, framing condition. Measured dimensions recorded. Mark density assessment. Provenance documentation reviewed. For hyper-realist works, a technical complexity assessment is conducted at this stage.

05

Research and Comparables

Comparables research from AstaGuru, Saffronart (Indian auction primary references), Christie’s, Sotheby’s, Bonhams (international auction references), gallery primary-market records for the specific artist, artist’s own secondary-market documentation, and — for emerging artists — documented social media and platform sales data. Scale and labour premium calculated. Hyper-realism premium assessed and expressed as a percentage uplift. CBDT Cost Inflation Index applied where the engagement is a capital-gain valuation.

06

Signed Valuation Report

The final ball pen painting valuation report on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, Government Approved Valuer, with certificate of registration. The report identifies: the artwork (artist, title, date, medium, dimensions, support); ink chemistry condition assessment; scale and labour documentation; hyper-realism premium (where applicable); drawing-vs-painting classification; comparables; methodology; basis of value; and concluded value. Accepted by the Income Tax Department, NCLT, the High Court, banks, and insurers. Digital and bound hard copy as standard.
