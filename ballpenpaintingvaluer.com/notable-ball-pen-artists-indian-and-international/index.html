Notable Ball Pen Artists — Indian and International Market Reference

The following artists represent the principal reference points for ball pen painting valuation comparables in the Indian and international markets. A2Z Valuers’ ball pen painting valuation practice maintains a continuously updated database of sale records for these and other ball pen artists to support valuation engagements where standard auction records are insufficient.

Indian Ball Pen Artists

Shashank Raste — among India’s most technically accomplished and widely recognised hyper-realist ball pen painters. Large-scale portrait and figure works executed in blue Biro on paper; gallery-represented in India with a documented collector market. Works by Raste represent the primary comparables reference for high-value Indian ball pen painting valuations.

Vilas Shinde — Indian hyper-realist ball pen practitioner known for large-scale figurative works; exhibited at Indian galleries and art fairs with a growing collector base.

CK Subramaniam — Indian artist known for intricate, highly detailed ball pen compositions; a significant practitioner in the Indian contemporary ball pen market.

Emerging Indian ball pen artists — a growing cohort of contemporary Indian artists working primarily or significantly in ball pen, represented through online platforms, Instagram, and Indian contemporary art galleries. For emerging artists without a formal auction record, gallery primary-market pricing and documented social media sales are the primary comparables sources.

International Ball Pen Artists

Juan Francisco Casas (Spain) — widely credited as the artist who first brought international critical attention to hyper-realist ball pen painting as a serious fine art practice; his large-scale figurative works in blue Biro have been exhibited internationally and have established auction records at Christie’s, Sotheby’s and Bonhams.

James Mylne (UK) — a leading British hyper-realist ball pen practitioner known for large-scale portraits and figure compositions; gallery-represented in the UK with a documented secondary market.

Lennie Mace (USA) — American ball pen artist known for intricate, multi-layered compositions; international exhibition record and critical coverage.

Alfredo Chamal — Italian ball pen artist known for technically demanding hyper-realist works; international gallery representation.

Additional international practitioners: A growing international cohort of ball pen practitioners across Europe, the Americas, Asia, and Africa, whose market positions range from emerging to established.

Note: A2Z Valuers does not authenticate artworks or certify attribution. Attribution assessments in valuation reports reflect the evidence available at the time of the valuation and do not constitute a certificate of authenticity.
