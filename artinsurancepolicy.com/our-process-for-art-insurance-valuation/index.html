Art Insurance Policy · India

From First Call to Issued Certificate — Our Five-Step Art Insurance Valuation Process

01

Identify the Insurance Need

Tell us what you are insuring, the policy type (Art Insurance Policy / Fine Arts Insurance Policy / Premium Collection Insurance), the coverage structure (scheduled / blanket / hybrid), and the valuation basis (agreed value / market value). We confirm the correct valuation methodology and quote fee and turnaround.

02

Engagement Letter

A written engagement letter setting out scope, valuation basis, deliverables, fee and turnaround. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

03

Inspection and Condition Report

Physical inspection of each insured work in situ. Photography under standard and raking light — documenting the work's condition at the date of the certificate. Condition notes identifying any pre-existing damage, conservation issues, or factors affecting value. This condition report is your baseline defence against exclusion disputes at claim time.

04

Research and Valuation

Comparables research from published auction records — Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Phillips for international; AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole's and DAG for Indian. Attribution and provenance review. Fair market value or replacement cost determination per work, in the format required by the insurer.

05

Issued Valuation Certificate

The valuation certificate is issued on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, in the format accepted by the insurer. Includes: work identification, description, condition summary, methodology, comparables, and concluded value. Digital PDF and bound hard copy. Accepted by HDFC ERGO, New India Assurance, Bajaj Allianz, ICICI Lombard, Tata AIG, United India, AXA XL, Chubb, and other leading art insurers.
