The Three Types of Art Insurance Policies — And the Valuation Each Requires

Type 1 — Art Insurance Policy

Type 1 — Art Insurance Policy (Individual Collector)

The Art Insurance Policy is designed for individual private collectors who own paintings, sculptures, antiques, rare books, or collectibles. It covers the collection whether at the collector's home, in storage, on loan to a gallery, or in transit. Coverage is typically on a wall-to-wall basis — from the moment the work leaves one location to the moment it arrives at and is installed in the next. The policy is structured around individually scheduled works, each with a separately agreed insured value supported by a valuation certificate.

The valuation A2Z Valuers provides for this policy type: Individual work-by-work valuation certificates establishing the fair market value or agreed replacement cost of each scheduled piece. Condition photographs under standard and raking light. Provenance documentation review. Updated every 2–3 years or when significant market movements occur.

Type 2 — Fine Arts Insurance Policy

Type 2 — Fine Arts Insurance Policy (Galleries, Dealers, Commercial Spaces)

The Fine Arts Insurance Policy is tailored for gallery owners, art dealers, auctioneers, curators, and other commercial art businesses. It covers the insured's entire inventory — including works purchased for resale, works received on consignment, and works on display to the public — against the full range of all-risks perils. Transit coverage is a particularly significant element for gallery and dealer policies, as works move frequently between premises, auction rooms, collectors, and exhibitions.

The policy typically provides blanket coverage at an agreed total value, with individual high-value works scheduled separately above the blanket threshold.

The valuation A2Z Valuers provides for this policy type: Portfolio-level valuation with individual scheduling for high-value works above the blanket threshold. Gallery inventory register creation. Transit valuation certificates for each major loan or consignment. Annual or biannual re-valuation for active gallery inventory.

Type 3 — Premium Collection Insurance

Type 3 — Premium Collection Insurance (Corporate and Institutional)

Premium Collection Insurance — the designation used by A2Z Valuers and several leading Indian insurers — covers corporate art collections, museum permanent collections, and institutional art holdings of significant scale and value. This policy type provides comprehensive protection for art held across multiple locations (multiple offices, exhibition venues, storage facilities) and typically includes extension coverage for newly acquired works (automatically covered for a specified period and percentage after acquisition, pending formal scheduling). The policy is structured around a master schedule of the entire collection, with quarterly or annual updates.

The valuation A2Z Valuers provides for this policy type: Full collection appraisal with asset register creation, individual valuations, aggregate portfolio value, and recommended insurance scheduling. Quarterly or annual update service for active collecting programmes. Accounting framework notes for balance-sheet alignment (Ind AS 16 / Ind AS 38).
