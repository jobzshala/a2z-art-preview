Art Insurance Valuation Services — A2Z Valuers

A2Z Valuers provides art insurance valuation certificates across six service contexts, each requiring a specific valuation methodology, a specific format, and a specific level of documentation to satisfy the insurer's requirements.

1. New Art Insurance Policy Valuation

The valuation certificate you need before your art insurance policy is issued. Establishes the fair market value or agreed replacement cost of each work to be insured, supporting the sum insured agreed between the policyholder and insurer. Includes: work-by-work assessment, condition report, provenance review, comparables from published auction records (Christie's, Sotheby's, Saffronart, AstaGuru, Pundole's and DAG), photography under standard and raking light, and a signed valuation certificate on A2Z Valuers letterhead accepted by leading Indian art insurers.

  • Turnaround: 24–72 hours for individual works; 5–10 working days for large collections
  • Typically engaged by: Private collectors, gallery owners, corporate art managers, artists

2. Policy Renewal Valuation

The updated valuation certificate required at each policy renewal cycle. For scheduled policies, values must be updated every 2–3 years — or sooner if significant market movements occur in the artist's market. For the Indian modern masters market in particular, benchmark sales at Christie's, Saffronart and AstaGuru have driven significant appreciation in recent years; a valuation certificate issued in 2020 for a Husain or Gaitonde work is materially undervalued at 2025–26 renewal.

Renewal without updating the sum insured exposes the policyholder to serious underinsurance risk. The Christie's sale of Husain's Untitled (Gram Yatra) at ₹119 crore on 19 March 2025 reset the ceiling for the entire Indian modern master market.

  • Turnaround: 24–72 hours for individual works; 5–10 working days for large collections
  • Typically engaged by: Existing policyholders approaching renewal; insurers requiring updated certificates

3. Claim Settlement Valuation

The most critical valuation service in art insurance — and the one where a Government Approved Valuer's independence matters most. When an art insurance claim is filed — for fire, flood, theft, accidental damage, or transit loss — the insurer appoints a surveyor to assess the loss. The policyholder's interest is served by an independent valuation from a Government Approved Valuer that establishes: (a) the fair market value of the lost or damaged work immediately before the loss; (b) the nature and extent of the damage in the event of partial loss; and (c) the cost of restoration to pre-loss condition, or the post-restoration residual value.

A2Z Valuers provides this independent claim-side valuation — defending the policyholder's position in what can otherwise be an unequal engagement with the insurer's surveyor.

  • Turnaround: 24–48 hours for urgent claim situations; call +91 9999992343
  • Typically engaged by: Policyholders in active claim; legal counsel advising on claim quantum; dispute resolution arbitrators

4. Transit Cover Valuation

Valuation certificates for wall-to-wall transit insurance — for artworks being moved for exhibition, sale, auction consignment, loan, conservation, or relocation. Transit is statistically among the highest-risk periods for fine art — transit-related damage accounts for up to 50% of fine art insurance claims globally. The transit valuation certificate establishes the insured value for each work during the specified transit, the origin and destination, the mode of transport, and any special handling requirements. For international transit, the certificate must address cross-border valuation equivalents.

  • Turnaround: 24–48 hours for most transit engagements
  • Typically engaged by: Collectors, galleries, auction houses, logistics providers, museums lending works

5. Gallery & Museum Insurance Valuation

Scheduled and blanket insurance valuations for commercial galleries, art dealers, and museums. Gallery inventory changes frequently — works sell, new works arrive on consignment, exhibitions rotate. A2Z Valuers provides: (a) an annual portfolio appraisal establishing the aggregate value for the blanket layer; (b) individual valuation certificates for works above the scheduling threshold; (c) exhibition coverage valuations for specific loan and exhibition events; and (d) consignment-receipt valuations for high-value works arriving from collectors or other institutions.

  • Typically engaged by: Gallery owners, art dealers, curators, museum registrars

6. Corporate Collection Insurance Valuation

Insurance valuations for corporate-owned art collections on commercial fine art policies or as scheduled additions to commercial property policies. Delivered as a complete collection appraisal with asset register, individual work valuations, aggregate insured value, and insurance scheduling recommendations in the format required by the corporate's insurer. For broader art asset appraisal for balance-sheet purposes, see artassetsappraiser.com.

  • Typically engaged by: CFOs, risk managers, company secretaries, corporate insurance brokers
