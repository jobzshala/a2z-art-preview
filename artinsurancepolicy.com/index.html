The critical link between valuation and insurance

Every Art Insurance Policy in India Requires a Professional Valuation Certificate. Here Is Why — And Why A2Z Valuers Provides It.

When you buy a fine art insurance policy in India, the insurer asks one question above all others before agreeing to cover your collection: what is the fair market value of each insured work? That value — established by a professional valuation certificate — determines the sum insured, the premium, and the settlement amount if a claim is made. Without a professional valuation certificate from a recognised, independent Government Approved Valuer, your art insurance policy rests on an unverifiable figure — and that figure will be disputed by the insurer the moment a claim is filed.

The standard art insurance policy document issued by HDFC ERGO, one of India's leading art insurance providers, states explicitly: "Fair Market Value to be agreed by Insured and us; duly supported with a valuation certificate from a professional valuer." Other leading Indian art insurers — New India Assurance, Bajaj Allianz, ICICI Lombard, Tata AIG, and United India — follow the same requirement. The valuation certificate is not optional. It is the contractual foundation of the policy. A2Z Valuers issues that certificate — signed by Nitesh Shrivastava, Government Approved Valuer — in a format accepted by every major art insurer operating in India.

Art Insurance Services

01

New Policy Valuation

Valuation certificates for new art insurance policies — establishing fair market value or replacement cost for each work, enabling the insurer to set the correct sum insured and the policyholder to avoid the twin risks of underinsurance and over-premium.

02

Policy Renewal Valuation

Updated valuation certificates at policy renewal — required every 2–3 years for scheduled policies as art market values fluctuate. An out-of-date sum insured exposes the collector to underinsurance risk; a current valuation ensures correct coverage.

03

Claim Settlement Valuation

Independent valuation for art insurance claim settlement — after fire, flood, theft, accidental damage, or transit loss. The claim quantum depends on the valuation methodology (agreed value vs market value) and the damage assessment. A2Z Valuers provides both.

04

Transit Cover Valuation

Valuation certificates for wall-to-wall transit insurance — for artworks being moved for exhibition, loan, sale, or relocation. Establishes the insurable value for domestic and international transit cover.

05

Gallery & Museum Insurance Valuation

Scheduled or blanket insurance valuations for gallery and museum collections — including works on loan, works in storage, works on public display, and works under conservation.

06

Corporate Collection Insurance Valuation

Insurance valuations for corporate-owned art collections — for companies insuring boardroom and office art under commercial fine art policies or scheduled additions to their property insurance.
