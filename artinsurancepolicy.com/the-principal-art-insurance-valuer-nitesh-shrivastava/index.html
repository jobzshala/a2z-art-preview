Why his valuation certificate is accepted

Nitesh Shrivastava's valuation certificates are accepted by every major art insurer operating in India — Indian domestic insurers (HDFC ERGO, New India Assurance, Bajaj Allianz, ICICI Lombard, Tata AIG, United India) and international insurers operating in India (AXA XL, Chubb, AIG, Lloyd's market syndicates) — because the certificate satisfies the requirements that insurers mandate: it is issued by a Government Approved Valuer registered under the Income Tax Act, it is signed personally by the principal valuer, it documents the methodology and comparables, and it establishes the value to the standard that the insurer's underwriting team and the surveyor's assessment framework require.

His art insurance valuation practice

Private collector policies — individual and scheduled valuation certificates for fine art collections held at primary residences, secondary residences, and in storage.

Gallery and dealer policies — annual portfolio appraisals for gallery inventory, consignment valuations, and exhibition cover certificates.

Museum and institutional policies — comprehensive collection appraisals for permanent collections, travelling exhibitions, and works received on loan.

Corporate collection policies — insurance valuations for corporate art programmes aligned with Ind AS accounting requirements.

Transit cover certificates — individual transit valuations for works moving domestically and internationally.

Claim settlement valuations — independent expert valuation in disputed or complex art insurance claims, including courtroom expert evidence.
