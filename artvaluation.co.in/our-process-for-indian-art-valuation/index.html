Art Valuation · India

From First Call to Signed Report — Our Six-Step Indian Art Valuation Process

01

Brief

A confidential conversation about the art asset, the Indian statute the report is being drafted under (Income Tax Act, IBC, Antiquities Act, insurance requirement), the authority the report is for (Income Tax Department, NCLT, bank, insurer, court), and the deadline. We confirm acceptance and indicate fee and timeline.

02

Engagement Letter

Written engagement letter setting out scope, statutory framework, basis of value, deliverable, fee, and turnaround. No work begins without a signed engagement letter. For urgent forensic and court matters, the engagement letter is issued simultaneously with the first substantive step.

03

Indian Statutory Framework Confirmation

The defining step of every Indian art valuation engagement. The applicable statutory framework is confirmed in writing: which Income Tax Act section governs the valuation, whether the CBDT Cost Inflation Index applies and for which years, whether the work is an antiquity subject to the AATA, whether IBBI Valuation Standards apply, and which Indian comparables databases are primary for this category and purpose. This written confirmation is shared with the client before inspection begins.

04

Physical Inspection

Physical inspection in situ. Photography under standard and raking light. Condition assessment — canvas, paper, textile, or other medium as applicable. Provenance documentation reviewed and recorded. For antiquities, ASI registration status verified.

05

Indian Comparables Research

Comparables research drawn from Indian auction records first — Saffronart, AstaGuru, Pundole’s, and DAG as primary Indian references; Christie’s and Sotheby’s Indian art sales as secondary international references. For folk and decorative arts, NGMA acquisition records, Crafts Council documentation, and specialist dealer records. CBDT Cost Inflation Index applied where the engagement is a capital-gain computation.

06

Final Signed Report

The final art valuation report issued on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, with the valuer's certificate of registration under the relevant Indian statute. Formatted specifically for the Indian authority that will read it. Digital and bound hard copy as standard.

Confidentiality

Every engagement is conducted under a strict confidentiality undertaking enforceable under Indian law. Photographs, valuation figures, and the existence of the engagement are not disclosed to any third party without written client consent.
