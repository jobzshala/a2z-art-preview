Arts Valuation Services — A2Z Valuers

A2Z Valuers accepts arts valuation engagements across six service lines — each applicable to any arts category the practice values, from a fine art painting to a folk art textile to a performing arts archive. Every report is prepared under the specific legal framework the purpose requires. The full directory of 250+ professional valuation services across India is at a2zvaluers.com.

1. Capital Gain & Income Tax Arts Valuation

Fair-market-value reports under the Income Tax Act for the sale of any arts asset — including 1 April 2001 base-date certificates under Section 55(2)(b) for legacy arts collections inherited or acquired before that date. The service covers fine art, folk art, decorative arts, literary assets, musical instruments, photography, and every other arts category. The Christie's sale of M.F. Husain's Untitled (Gram Yatra) at ₹119 crore on 19 March 2025 is among the benchmark comparables the practice tracks for Indian modern art capital-gain computations; for folk art and decorative arts categories, specialist comparables databases and institutional acquisition records are used. For the dedicated capital-gain practice, see capitalgainvaluation.com and incometaxvaluation.com.

  • Typically engaged by: Sellers, CAs, tax counsel, NRI clients, estate executors

2. Insurance Arts Valuation

Replacement-cost valuation reports for arts insurance policies — for new policy underwriting, policy renewal, and post-loss claim settlement. The service covers all arts categories: the scheduled fine art policy that insures a Husain canvas, the blanket policy that covers a folk art foundation's collection, the transit cover for performing arts memorabilia being moved between cities, and the institutional policy that protects a literary archive.

Condition assessment is conducted under standard and raking light, with photography documenting baseline condition at the date of the certificate. For the full insurance advisory, see artinsurancepolicy.com and insurancevaluation.co.in.

  • Typically engaged by: Insurers, brokers, collectors, galleries, folk art foundations, literary archives

3. Estate & Family Division Arts Valuation

Equitable-distribution valuations for inheritance, partition, family settlement, and probate of arts collections across all arts categories. The challenge in arts estate work is precisely the breadth of the category: a single family collection may include oil paintings by a known Indian modern master, a folk art textile collection from the family's regional tradition, autographed books from a literary estate, and vintage family photographs that have acquired fine-art status.

A2Z Valuers issues a single integrated valuation report covering all arts categories in the estate, with each category valued under its own methodology and comparables standard. For art assets in IBC or larger estate proceedings, see also artassetsappraiser.com.

  • Typically engaged by: Executors, beneficiaries, family offices, estate counsel, probate courts

4. Auction & Pre-Sale Arts Valuation

Independent second opinions on auction-house pre-sale estimates before consignment to Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Phillips, AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole's and DAG — for any arts category those houses handle. For folk art and performing arts memorabilia being considered for specialist or general auction, the pre-sale valuation establishes an independent reserve-setting reference before the auction house's own estimate is accepted. For broader auction advisory, see a2zauctionhouse.com.

  • Typically engaged by: Consignors, family offices, estate executors, arts foundations, dealers

5. Donation & Museum Gift Arts Valuation

Donation valuations for gifts of arts assets to museums, cultural trusts, educational institutions, and archives — for the NGMA (New Delhi and Mumbai), CSMVS (Mumbai), National Museum, state museums, folk art foundations, craft documentation archives, literary archives, and film heritage institutions. The valuation must satisfy the donee institution's formal acquisition requirements and the donor's Indian tax position. For the full donation valuation practice, see giftvaluation.com.

  • Typically engaged by: Donors, donee institutions, museum acquisition teams, charitable trusts, arts foundations

6. Litigation & Forensic Arts Valuation

Reports for courts, tribunals, IT and GST search-and-seizure proceedings, customs assessments, matrimonial disputes, and arbitration — for all arts categories. Forensic arts valuation is a particularly challenging discipline: the valuer must not only establish fair market value but must do so under the evidentiary standards of the forum, with methodology that a court-appointed expert or opposing counsel can scrutinize and that the judge or tribunal can understand.

Nitesh Shrivastava is registered under Sections 132(2) and 132(9D) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for forensic valuation and has been appointed by government agencies for PAN India arts assessment during enforcement proceedings.

  • Typically engaged by: Counsel, courts, IT/GST/Customs respondents, matrimonial litigants, arbitrators
