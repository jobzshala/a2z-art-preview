Arts Valuation · India

From First Call to Signed Certificate — Our Six-Step Arts Valuation Process

01

Brief

A confidential conversation about the arts asset — what it is, the arts category it belongs to, approximate age or date, known provenance, purpose of the valuation (capital gain, insurance, estate, auction, donation, litigation), and the deadline. We confirm acceptance and indicate fee and timeline.

02

Engagement Letter

Written engagement letter setting out scope, applicable statutory framework (Income Tax Act, IBC, Antiquities Act as applicable), basis of value, deliverable, fee, and turnaround. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

03

Category-Specific Protocol Selection

The first analytical act for every engagement — determining which arts category the asset belongs to and selecting the appropriate valuation protocol. A Gond painting is not valued like a Husain canvas. A vintage sitar is not valued like a Mughal miniature. Each arts category has its own primary value drivers, comparables sources, condition assessment protocol, and provenance framework. This step is documented in writing and confirmed with the client before physical inspection begins.

04

Physical Inspection

Physical inspection in situ. Photography under standard and raking light; for manuscripts and works on paper, additional transmitted-light examination where appropriate. Category-specific condition assessment — canvas condition for paintings, textile integrity for folk art, surface oxidation for metalwork, mechanical function for musical instruments, print quality and paper condition for photographs, digital-file integrity for digital arts. Provenance documentation reviewed.

05

Research & Comparables

Category-specific comparables assembly: Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Phillips for international auction; AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole's and DAG for Indian auction; NGMA acquisition records and Crafts Council documentation for folk arts; Bonhams Musical Instruments for instruments; manuscripts sales records for literary arts; platform records for digital arts. Attribution analysis, scholarly literature review, and institutional precedent research.

06

Signed Valuation Report

Final valuation report on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, with valuer's certificate of registration. Identifies the arts asset, category, condition, provenance, methodology, comparables, and final value. Accepted by the Income Tax Department, NCLT, the High Court, insurers, embassies, and auction houses. Digital and bound hard copies as standard.
