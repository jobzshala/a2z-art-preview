Artwork Valuation · India

Artwork Valuation Across Every Indian State and Union Territory

A2Z Valuers delivers PAN India artwork valuation — with physical inspection, Indian comparables research, and report delivery handled by the operating team from the Connaught Place, New Delhi headquarters, with trusted associates in major metros. Every artwork valuation report is signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, regardless of the city in which the artwork is situated.

Delhi NCR — India’s Art Administration Hub

Delhi is India’s art market administration capital — home to the National Museum, NGMA New Delhi, Archaeological Survey of India headquarters, Income Tax Department headquarters, and major galleries including Dhoomimal Gallery (est. 1936, India’s oldest), DAG, Vadehra, Palette, and Art Alive. A2Z Valuers is headquartered in Connaught Place, New Delhi and serves Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Gurgaon, and Gurugram for all artwork valuation purposes.

Mumbai — India’s Largest Artwork Market

Mumbai is the commercial hub of the Indian art market — home to AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole’s, Christie’s India representative office, CSMVS (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya), and galleries including Chemould Prescott Road, Sakshi, Jhaveri Contemporary, and Pundole’s Art Gallery. For Income Tax, insurance, estate, and IBC artwork valuation purposes in Mumbai, A2Z Valuers operates with the same Indian comparables-first methodology and the same Government Approved signature that serves Delhi.

Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad & South India

South India’s artwork market encompasses Gallery Sumukha (Bengaluru), Apparao Galleries (Chennai), Sista’s Art Gallery (Chennai), and a significant collector base for South Indian classical painting, bronze sculpture, and contemporary Indian art. Kolkata’s historic Bengal School collector community, Pune’s growing contemporary art market, Ahmedabad’s active collector base, and Jaipur’s Rajasthani art tradition — all served with on-ground inspection and the same Indian-statute-compliant report quality.

All States & Union Territories

All 28 States and 8 Union Territories of India — including Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities where collectors of regional folk art traditions, decorative arts, and cultural property require Government Approved artwork valuation reports for insurance, inheritance, and income-tax purposes. Travel and on-ground inspection arranged by the operating team without inconveniencing the client.
