The India-first proposition

A2Z Valuers is a Delhi-headquartered valuation practice led by Nitesh Shrivastava — Civil Engineer, Government Approved Valuer, and Proprietor. artworkvaluation.in is the India-domestic site of this practice — the single-window platform for Indian assessees, Chartered Accountants, advocates, bank credit officers, Resolution Professionals, and Indian courts who need an artwork valuation report prepared under Indian law by an Indian Government Approved Valuer. What Indian statutory precision means in artwork valuation

Statute-specific compliance — every artwork valuation report identifies the Indian statute under which it is issued, the section it complies with, and the assumptions specific to that section.

CBDT Cost Inflation Index — cited in every capital-gain artwork valuation, with the specific Index for the year of acquisition and the year of sale stated in the report body.

Section 50C reconciliation — for capital-gain computations where the artwork sale also involves a registered property, the stamp-duty value is reconciled with the fair market value evidence.

IBBI Valuation Standards and Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules, 2017 — applied for every IBC and Companies Act artwork valuation report.

Indian auction-house comparables first — for an Indian IT assessment, AstaGuru and Saffronart data is the primary reference; Christie’s London data is secondary. The practice sequences comparables accordingly in every report.

Signed and stamped reports on letterhead with the principal valuer’s Government Approved Valuer certificate of registration.
