From First Contact to Certified Appraisal Report — Our Seven-Step Process

01

Brief

A confidential conversation about the artwork (what it is, by whom, approximate age and medium), the purpose of the appraisal, the authority or institution the report is for, and the deadline. We confirm the applicable appraisal standard (USPAP, IVSC, RICS Red Book, or Indian statutory), indicate the fee and turnaround, and confirm acceptance.

02

Engagement Letter

A written engagement letter setting out the scope, the appraisal standard, the basis of value, the deliverable, the fee, and the turnaround. The engagement letter includes our independence declaration and our confidentiality undertaking. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

03

Documentation Review

Review of all available documentation before physical inspection: prior appraisal reports, auction catalogues or receipts, gallery invoices, exhibition catalogues, provenance documentation, conservation reports, and insurance certificates. Gaps in the documentation record are noted and their significance assessed before inspection.

04

Physical Inspection

Physical inspection of the artwork in situ, conducted personally by Nitesh Shrivastava. Photography under standard and raking light; ultraviolet examination where relevant. Condition assessment completed on a standardised form. Dimensions verified. Signature and inscription documented. Verso examined and all labels, stamps, and annotations recorded.

05

Research & Comparables

Attribution research against published scholarly sources and catalogue raisonnés where available. Provenance verification against exhibition and auction records. Comparables assembly from Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Phillips, Saffronart, AstaGuru, Pundole's and DAG — with specific comparables selected for similarity of artist, period, medium, condition, and size. Analysis of each comparable against the subject artwork, with adjustments documented.

06

Report Drafting & Internal Review

The appraisal report is drafted incorporating all ten required elements (identification, condition, provenance, attribution, methodology, comparables, concluded value, limiting conditions, and certification). Internal review for factual accuracy, methodology consistency, and standard compliance before the report is shared with the client. Client is given the opportunity to provide factual corrections (not value corrections) before the final report is signed.

07

Certified Appraisal Report

The final certified appraisal report is issued on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, with the valuer's certificate of registration, the appraiser certification, and all required disclosures under the applicable appraisal standard. Digital PDF and bound hard copy as standard. Accepted by Indian courts, NCLT, ITAT, insurers, museums, embassies, and international auction houses.
