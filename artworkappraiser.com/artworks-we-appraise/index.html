The Artworks A2Z Valuers Appraises — By Medium and Category

Paintings

Oil on canvas, oil on board, acrylic on canvas, watercolour, gouache, tempera, fresco, encaustic, pastel — across all Indian and international art historical periods. Indian modern masters (Husain, Souza, Raza, Gaitonde, Tyeb Mehta), Bengal School (Tagore, Nandalal Bose, Jamini Roy), Mughal and Rajput court painting, contemporary Indian art, European old masters. For dedicated painting appraisal, see paintingvaluers.com and the 50+ medium-specialist sister sites in the network.

Sculptures

Bronze, stone, terracotta, wood, ivory, and mixed-media sculpture — including South Indian temple bronzes (Chola, Vijayanagara, Nayaka), North Indian stone sculpture (Gandharan, Mathura, Nagara), contemporary Indian sculpture (Ravinder Reddy, Subodh Gupta), and international sculpture. Attribution, material analysis, and provenance are primary appraisal factors for Indian bronze sculpture, where temple theft and repatriation risk are active considerations.

Works on Paper

Drawings, prints, lithographs, etchings, engravings, woodcuts, pastels, watercolours, and works in mixed media on paper — including Indian miniature paintings on paper and ivory, Kalighat paintings, Company School watercolours, and works on paper by Indian modern masters. For engravings and lithographs, see engravingslithgographsvaluers.com.

Photographs

Fine-art photographic prints by Indian and international photographers — Raghu Rai, Pablo Bartholomew, Dayanita Singh, Sooni Taraporevala, and others. Edition number, print date, print size, and condition are primary appraisal factors. Vintage photographic prints from the colonial and early post-independence period are additionally subject to provenance assessment. For dedicated photography appraisal, see photographsvaluers.com.

Folk and Tribal Art

Madhubani, Warli, Gond, Phad, Kalamkari, Kalighat, Pichwai, Patachitra, Kerala Murals, Thangka, Tanjore, Mysore — India's rich tradition of folk and tribal art, appraised by A2Z Valuers with reference to artist identity and community, condition, format, and institutional acquisition records.

Decorative Arts & Objects

Ceramics, glass, metalwork (silver, bronze, brass, bidriware), textiles, carpets, furniture, jade, hardstone, and ivory — appraised under the decorative-arts methodology that accounts for maker, period, condition, and the specific market for each sub-category. For decorative art and furniture appraisal, see decorativeartfurniturevaluers.com.

Digital & New Media Art

NFT-based artworks, video art, generative art, and digital photographs — appraised with appropriate methodology disclosure and with explicit acknowledgement that this category's market is volatile and the methodology is evolving. Edition documentation, smart-contract records, and platform sales history are the primary data sources. For digital art appraisal, see digitalpaintingvaluer.com.
