Artworks the Registered Government Approved Artwork Valuer Values — Every Category, Every Medium

Indian Modern Masters

Progressive Artists’ Group and peers — Husain, Souza, Raza, Gaitonde, Tyeb Mehta, Krishen Khanna, Ram Kumar, Akbar Padamsee, Bhupen Khakhar. The highest-liquidity artwork category in India. Deepest Indian auction comparables record — AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole’s, DAG. See modernartvaluers.com and indianmordernartvaluers.com.

Bengal School & Pre-Modern Indian Art

Tagore, Nandalal Bose, Jamini Roy, Abanindranath Tagore and the Bengal School. Significant Kolkata collector base; frequent 1 April 2001 base-date valuation requests for Income Tax purposes.

Contemporary Indian Art

Subodh Gupta, Atul Dodiya, Jitish Kallat, Bharti Kher, Jagannath Panda and the broader contemporary Indian scene. Active secondary market on AstaGuru and Saffronart; primary-market gallery data from DAG, Vadehra, Chemould, Sakshi, Apparao. See contemporaryartvaluers.com.

Mughal, Rajput & Historical Indian Paintings

Court miniatures, Mughal, Deccani, Rajput, Pahari, and Company School. Antiquities Act compliance dimension for objects over 100 years old. Christie’s and Sotheby’s as primary international comparables; AstaGuru for Indian data. See artantiquesvaluers.com.

Folk & Tribal Indian Art

Madhubani, Warli, Gond, Phad, Kalamkari, Kalighat, Pichwai, Patachitra, Kerala Murals. NGMA acquisition records, Crafts Council documentation, and AstaGuru folk art auctions as primary comparables. A2Z Valuers values folk and tribal art under the same rigorous registered-valuer methodology applied to fine art.

Decorative Arts, Photographs, Manuscripts & Digital Art

Ceramics, silver, textiles, carpets, Anglo-Indian furniture, and hardstone; fine-art photography by Raghu Rai, Dayanita Singh, and the Indian photography market; manuscripts and literary archives; and digital arts including NFT-based works. For the full category coverage, see artsvaluation.com.
