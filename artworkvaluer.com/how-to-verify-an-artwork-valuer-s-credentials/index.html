How to Verify an Artwork Valuer’s Credentials — The Checklist for Indian Clients

Before commissioning an artwork valuation report, verify the valuer’s credentials against this checklist. Every item below is verifiable against public records or by direct request to the valuer. A registered Government Approved Artwork Valuer will provide documentation for every item without hesitation. An unregistered opinion-giver will not be able to do so.

The credential verification checklist

The credential verification checklist (ten items)

Section 34AB registration: Ask for the valuer’s Section 34AB registration certificate. It should identify the registered valuer by name, the asset class (artworks and cultural assets), the registration number, and the renewal date. Verify the registration is current and not expired.

Rule 13 renewal compliance: Confirm the registration has been renewed as required under Rule 13. A registration that has lapsed is not a current registration.

IBBI registration (for IBC engagements): For Regulation 27 CIRP artwork valuations, confirm the valuer is on the current IBBI Registered Valuers panel. The IBBI publishes its panel on its website; verify the valuer’s name appears.

Government agency appointments: Ask whether the valuer has been appointed by the Income Tax Department, GST Department, Narcotics Department, or Customs Department for artwork valuations. Government appointments are the strongest endorsement of a registered valuer’s competence.

Bank empanelment: For bank-collateral and SARFAESI engagements, confirm the valuer is on the relevant bank’s empanelled-valuer list. The bank’s credit department can confirm this.

Asset class specificity: Confirm the registration is specifically for artworks and cultural assets — not a general valuer’s registration that lists artworks as an add-on category. An artwork valuation requires a valuer whose primary registered asset class is artworks.

Independence declaration: Ask the valuer to confirm in writing that they hold no financial interest in the transaction and that no commercial relationship with any party to the transaction exists that could compromise their independence.

Conduct record: Ask whether the valuer has been subject to any Section 34AB deregistration proceeding, IBBI disciplinary action, or court finding of negligence or misstatement in a valuation report. A registered valuer with a clean record will confirm this in writing without hesitation.

Methodology disclosure: Ask the valuer to identify upfront the methodology they will apply to the specific artwork, the comparables sources they will use, and the basis of value appropriate to the purpose. A competent registered artwork valuer can answer all three questions before inspection.

Report format sample: Ask to see a redacted sample of a report issued for a similar purpose (capital gain, insurance, IBC). The format, the methodology disclosure, the comparables table, and the certification language should all be visible. If the valuer cannot or will not provide a sample, treat that as a material concern.
