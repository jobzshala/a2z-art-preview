Engaging the Registered Artwork Valuer — From Credential Verification to Signed Report

Engaging a registered Government Approved Artwork Valuer is a formal professional engagement — not an informal inquiry. It follows a defined process that begins with credential verification (because we believe the client has the right to verify before they engage) and ends with a signed, stamped report that carries the full statutory authority of the registered valuer’s credential. Every step is documented.

01

Credential Verification

Before engagement, verify the valuer’s credentials. Use the ten-item checklist on the Credentials Verification page. Nitesh Shrivastava’s Section 34AB registration, IBBI registration, government appointments, and bank empanelments are disclosed in full on this site. Request the registration documentation; it will be provided without hesitation.

02

Initial Brief

A confidential conversation about the artwork, the purpose of the valuation (capital gain, insurance, estate, IBC, forensic, pre-sale), the Indian authority the report is for, and the deadline. The valuer identifies the applicable statutory framework, the basis of value, and the methodology to be applied — upfront, in plain terms, before the engagement letter is signed.

03

Engagement Letter

Written engagement letter identifying: the valuer (Nitesh Shrivastava, registration details), the artwork, the statutory framework, the basis of value, the deliverable, the fee, and the turnaround. The engagement letter makes the valuer’s accountability explicit — it identifies who is signing the report and what they are accountable for delivering. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

04

Physical Inspection

Physical inspection of the artwork in situ by Nitesh Shrivastava or a qualified associate under his direct supervision. Photography, condition assessment, and provenance review. The registered valuer’s personal involvement in the inspection is what makes the signed report his personal accountability.

05

Research, Comparables & Methodology

Comparables research from AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole’s, DAG, Christie’s, and Sotheby’s as appropriate to the artwork category. CBDT Cost Inflation Index applied for capital-gain engagements. Methodology selected, documented, and applied consistently with the statutory framework.

06

Signed Report Delivery

The final artwork valuation report on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed by Nitesh Shrivastava, Government Approved Valuer, with registration number and official stamp. The registered valuer’s signature on the report is not a formality. It is the assertion of personal professional accountability for every figure in the document. Digital and bound hard copy as standard.
