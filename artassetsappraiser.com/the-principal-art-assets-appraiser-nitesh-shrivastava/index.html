Art asset appraisal engagement profile

Nitesh Shrivastava's art asset appraisal engagement record spans every institutional context that matters:

Corporate art collections — appraisal and asset-register creation for Indian corporates and MNCs operating in India.

Art-backed lending — collateral appraisals for private banks and NBFCs advancing credit against Indian modern art and antiquities portfolios.
