The shift: from art as object to art as asset

Art Has Moved from the Drawing Room to the Balance Sheet. Appraisal Must Follow.

The Indian art market has undergone a fundamental shift in the last two decades. Art is no longer only a cultural asset or a passion purchase. For a growing segment of the Indian market — high-net-worth individuals, family offices, corporate collectors, private banks, wealth managers, and institutional investors — art is a managed financial asset: one that appears on balance sheets, is pledged as loan collateral, is held in IBC estates, is managed as part of diversified investment portfolios, and generates — or should generate — scheduled tax and reporting obligations. The appraisal discipline that serves this market is different from the one that serves individual collectors. It does not stop at "this painting is worth ₹X." It addresses: what is the art asset's contribution to net worth, what is its collateral value for a bank, what is its fair value or liquidation value in a CIRP proceeding, what is its insurance replacement cost for a scheduled fine art policy, what is its capital-gain exposure on realisation, and what is the acquisition due diligence that should precede a purchase decision at this asset level.

A2Z Valuers is built for exactly this level of engagement. The practice brings over two decades of Government Approved valuation experience to every art asset appraisal — with reports accepted by the Income Tax Department, NCLT, NCLAT, DRT, private banks, NBFCs, and leading insurers. When the question "who is India's leading Government Approved Art Assets Appraiser" is asked, this is the practice the answer points to.

Art Assets Appraisal Services

01

Corporate Art Collection Appraisal

Scheduled appraisal of corporate-owned art collections for balance-sheet inclusion, fixed-asset revaluation, depreciation analysis, boardroom and office collections. Issued under Ind AS 16 (Property, Plant and Equipment) or Ind AS 38 (Intangible Assets) framework as applicable.

02

Art as Loan Collateral Appraisal

Appraisal reports for art-backed lending — accepted by private banks and NBFCs advancing credit against art portfolios. Establishes collateral value, liquidity assessment, and recommended loan-to-value ratio for the lender's credit decision.

03

Art Assets in IBC / NCLT Proceedings

Fair value and liquidation value appraisals for art assets held by corporate debtors in CIRP proceedings — for Resolution Professionals, the Committee of Creditors, NCLT and NCLAT, under Regulation 27 of the IBC CIRP Regulations.

04

Family Office Art Portfolio Appraisal

Comprehensive portfolio-level appraisal for family offices managing multi-generational art collections — with asset register, individual work valuations, aggregate portfolio value, liquidity tiers, and insurance scheduling recommendations.

05

Capital Gain & Income Tax Appraisal

Fair-market-value certificates under the Income Tax Act for realisation of art assets — including 1 April 2001 base-date appraisals for legacy collections and indexed cost computation. See capitalgainvaluation.com.

06

Insurance Appraisal for Art Assets

Replacement-cost appraisals for scheduled fine art insurance policies — and for claim settlement after fire, theft, flood, or transit damage. Includes condition assessment, provenance, and published auction comparables. See insurancevaluation.co.in.

07

Art Acquisition Due Diligence Appraisal

Pre-acquisition appraisal and due diligence for institutional and corporate art buyers — establishing independent fair-market value, attribution assessment, provenance verification, and Antiquities Act compliance status before the purchase price is committed.
