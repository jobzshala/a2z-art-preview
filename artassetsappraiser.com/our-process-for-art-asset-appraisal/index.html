From Enquiry to Appraisal Report — Our Seven-Step Art Asset Appraisal Process

01

Scope Definition

A confidential engagement-scoping conversation identifying: the nature and scale of the art asset portfolio, the purpose (corporate balance sheet / loan collateral / IBC / family office / capital gain / insurance / acquisition due diligence), the audience for the report (CFO / bank / NCLT / insurer / tax authority), and the deadline. We confirm scope, fee and timeline.

02

Engagement Letter

A written engagement letter setting out scope, applicable statutory framework, deliverables (asset register format, individual appraisals, aggregate summary, collateral schedule, insurance schedule as applicable), fee and turnaround. No work begins without a signed engagement letter.

03

Asset Register Creation

For portfolio-level engagements, a complete asset register is created before appraisal work begins — cataloguing each work by artist, title, year, medium, dimensions, acquisition cost, acquisition date, current location, and existing insurance / valuation documentation. The register is the backbone of every subsequent deliverable.

04

Physical Inspection

Physical inspection of each art asset, in situ where possible. Photography under standard and raking light. Condition assessment noting any conservation issues, restoration evidence, or material deterioration that affects value. For corporate collections, location and display condition (lighting, humidity, security) are noted as material risk factors for insurance.

05

Research & Comparables

Category-specific comparables from Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Phillips for international auction; AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole's and DAG for Indian auction and primary market. Attribution analysis, provenance review, and Antiquities Act classification where relevant. For collateral and IBC appraisals, liquidation value estimation from distress-sale comparables and dealer buy-price data.

06

Financial Asset Analysis

For corporate, collateral, IBC and portfolio engagements: liquidity tier classification of each work and the portfolio; loan-to-value recommendations for collateral engagements; accounting framework notes for corporate balance sheet engagements; fair value and liquidation value computation for IBC Regulation 27 compliance.

07

Signed Appraisal Report

The final appraisal report issued on A2Z Valuers letterhead, signed personally by Nitesh Shrivastava, with the valuer's certificate of registration, in the format required by the specific audience (CFO / bank / NCLT / insurer / tax authority). Digital and bound hard copies as standard.

Confidentiality

Every art asset appraisal engagement is conducted under the practice's most stringent confidentiality protocols. Portfolio details, individual valuations, and client identity are not disclosed to any third party without written consent. For family office and private-client engagements, this standard is absolute.
