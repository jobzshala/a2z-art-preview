Why artwork evaluations matter

An Artwork Without a Current Evaluation Is an Asset Without a Value. And an Asset Without a Value Is a Liability Waiting to Happen.

Every artwork in a collection — however casually acquired, however long held, however modest or significant — is a legal asset. It can be seized by the Income Tax Department and assessed at a value the assessee has no certified figure to contest. It can be destroyed by fire or flood without adequate insurance because the sum insured was set years ago against an outdated evaluation. It can become the subject of a family dispute in which no party has a credible independent figure to negotiate from. It can be donated to a museum without the documentation the institution requires. A current, Government Approved artwork evaluation is the document that prevents each of these outcomes. It is not a luxury for large collections or valuable artworks. It is the minimum responsible management of any art asset, at any value level.

A2Z Valuers, under Nitesh Shrivastava, conducts artwork evaluations for individual collectors with a single painting, corporate art managers overseeing hundreds of works, museums conducting periodic collection reviews, Resolution Professionals administering corporate debtors’ estates, and families dividing collections in inheritance. The evaluation format, the methodology, and the comparables change with the purpose and the artwork. The standard of independence, documentation, and defensibility does not.

Evaluation Types

01

Single Artwork Evaluation

A focused, object-level evaluation of one artwork — condition assessment, provenance research, attribution analysis, comparables, and concluded value. Issued as a stand-alone evaluation certificate for any single purpose. The foundation service.

02

Collection & Portfolio Evaluation

A systematic evaluation of an entire art collection or portfolio — from a family collection of 20 works to a gallery inventory of 200. Each work evaluated individually; a consolidated portfolio report issued with aggregate values by artist, category, and date.

03

Corporate Art Collection Evaluation

Evaluation of corporate-owned art displayed across offices, boardrooms, reception areas, and client-facing spaces — for balance-sheet reporting under Ind AS 16 / Ind AS 38, insurance coverage, and asset management. Issued as a structured corporate evaluation schedule.

04

Institutional & Museum Evaluation

Collection evaluation for museums, cultural trusts, universities, and public institutions — for accreditation, insurance, grant applications, donation acceptance, and de-accession. Formatted to satisfy institutional governance and audit requirements.

05

Pre-Purchase Evaluation

An independent evaluation before a purchase commitment — verifying the asking price against market comparables, assessing condition risks, and flagging provenance concerns before the buyer is committed. The evaluation that pays for itself on the first discrepancy it catches.

06

Periodic Re-Evaluation Programme

A structured schedule of regular artwork re-evaluations — annual, biennial, or on an event-triggered basis — keeping insurance coverage current, estate planning documentation up to date, and corporate asset registers accurate as the art market moves.

Evaluation Purposes

Every artwork evaluation A2Z Valuers issues serves a specific legal or financial purpose. The purpose determines the basis of value, the statutory framework, and the format of the evaluation report.

Capital Gain & Income Tax — fair market value under the Income Tax Act, including 1 April 2001 base-date evaluations under Section 55(2)(b) and Cost Inflation Index computation.

Insurance — replacement-cost evaluation for artwork insurance policies; accepted by leading Indian and international insurers.

Estate & Family Division — equitable-distribution evaluations for inheritance, partition, and probate across Indian courts.

Auction & Pre-Sale — independent evaluation before consignment to Christie's, Sotheby's, AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole's, or DAG.

Charitable Donation — donation evaluations for gifts to museums, cultural trusts, and educational institutions.

Litigation & Forensic — evaluation reports for courts, NCLT, ITAT, DRT, IT search-and-seizure, and matrimonial disputes.

Corporate & IBC — Ind AS-compliant evaluations for balance-sheet reporting; Regulation 27 fair-value and liquidation-value evaluations for IBC CIRP proceedings.
