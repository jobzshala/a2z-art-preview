Artwork Evaluations · India

Artworks A2Z Valuers Evaluates — Across Every Medium, Style, and Tradition

Indian Modern & Contemporary Paintings

The highest-liquidity category in the Indian art market — Progressive Artists’ Group (Husain, Souza, Raza, Gaitonde, Tyeb Mehta, Krishen Khanna, Ram Kumar, Akbar Padamsee), Bengal School (Tagore, Nandalal Bose, Jamini Roy), and contemporary Indian artists (Subodh Gupta, Atul Dodiya, Jitish Kallat, Bharti Kher). Evaluation comparables drawn from AstaGuru, Saffronart, Pundole’s, DAG, Christie’s and Sotheby’s. For dedicated modern and contemporary evaluation, see modernartvaluers.com and contemporaryartvaluers.com.

Mughal, Rajput & Historical Indian Paintings

Court miniatures, Mughal, Deccani, Rajput, Pahari, and Company School paintings. These are antiquities under the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, 1972 for objects over 100 years old, adding a compliance dimension to every evaluation. Comparables from Christie’s and Sotheby’s Islamic and Indian Art sales; AstaGuru for Indian auction data. For the dedicated practice, see artantiquesvaluers.com.

Folk & Tribal Indian Art

Madhubani, Warli, Gond, Phad, Kalamkari, Kalighat, Pichwai, Patachitra, Kerala Murals, Tanjore, Mysore, and other regional traditions. Evaluation comparables from NGMA acquisition records, Crafts Council documentation, specialist dealer records, and AstaGuru’s folk art auctions. A2Z Valuers evaluates folk and tribal art under the same rigorous standards applied to fine art — including individual artist attribution, condition assessment, and medium-specific evaluation protocol.

Sculptures, Drawings, Prints & Works on Paper

Three-dimensional works in bronze, stone, terracotta, and mixed media; drawings, watercolours, pastels, and gouaches; limited-edition prints, etchings, lithographs, and engravings. Edition-number documentation is a primary value factor for prints. For dedicated print and engraving evaluation, see engravingslithgographsvaluers.com.

Decorative Arts, Textiles & Carpets

Ceramics, glass, silver and metalwork, jade and hardstone, Anglo-Indian colonial furniture, Mughal and Indo-Persian carpets, Kashmiri shawls, Zardozi, Paithani and Kanchipuram textiles. For dedicated evaluation, see decorativeartfurniturevaluers.com, rugscarpetsvaluation.com, and textilesvaluation.com.

Photography, Manuscripts & Literary Arts

Fine-art photographic prints by Raghu Rai, Dayanita Singh, Pablo Bartholomew and the Indian photography market; manuscripts in Persian, Sanskrit, Urdu, and regional languages; first editions, signed books, and author correspondence. For photography, see photographsvaluers.com; for manuscripts, see manuscriptsvaluation.com.

Digital Art & New Media

NFT-based works, video art, generative art, and digital photography in limited editions. Evaluation methodology for digital arts applies a framework adapted from the print-edition market, with blockchain ownership records and platform sales data as primary comparables. Uncertainty in this category is explicitly disclosed in every evaluation report. For digital painting, see digitalpaintingvaluer.com.
