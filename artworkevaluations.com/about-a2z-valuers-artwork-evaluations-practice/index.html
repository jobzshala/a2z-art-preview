A2Z Valuers Artwork Evaluations — Independent, Documented, Defensible

What the practice does

A2Z Valuers artwork evaluations practice, led by Nitesh Shrivastava — Government Approved Valuer, conducts systematic, independent, and documented artwork evaluations for clients across India and internationally. The practice is process-built: every evaluation follows a defined protocol that adapts to the artwork category and the evaluation purpose, but never compromises on the four qualities that make an artwork evaluation defensible — independence (no commercial interest in the outcome), documentation (every figure supported by evidence), methodology (every approach identified and disclosed), and accountability (the evaluator’s signature is the evaluator’s reputation).

The practice operates across 250+ professional valuation services across India, from a single painting evaluation for a first-time collector to a structured annual re-evaluation programme for a corporate art collection of several hundred works.

Why “evaluations” — the practice philosophy

The word “evaluation” carries a specific connotation in the art market: the act of assessing and determining value through a structured process, rather than merely issuing a certificate. A2Z Valuers uses this word deliberately. An artwork evaluation is not a one-page letter of opinion. It is a documented investigation — physical examination, condition assessment, provenance research, attribution analysis, comparables assembly, methodology selection, and value conclusion — that produces a report that can be submitted to a court, a tax authority, an insurer, a board of trustees, or an auction house and withstand the scrutiny each of those audiences applies. That is what A2Z Valuers delivers across every engagement, regardless of scale.

Evaluation standards applied

USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice) — the international evaluation standard applied for cross-border engagements and where the client’s insurer or counterparty specifies USPAP compliance.

IVSC IVS 2022 (International Valuation Standards Council) — applied for IBC and Companies Act evaluations, where IBBI-aligned methodology is required.

RICS Red Book (Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors) — applied for UK-counterparty engagements and where institutional clients specify RICS-standard evaluations.

Income Tax Act, 1961 — Section 34AB — the Indian registered-valuer qualification under which every Indian statutory artwork evaluation is issued.

IBBI Valuation Standards — applied for Regulation 27 CIRP fair-value and liquidation-value artwork evaluations under the IBC.
